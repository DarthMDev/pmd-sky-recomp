Use DEFAULT_TILE as a struct address, not a pointer slot.\
\
In a lit dungeon at 21:9 with camera X at zero, the left margin samples outside the tile grid. DEFAULT_TILE is a zero-filled struct tile. Reading its first word as a pointer yields zero, so ReadEntry rejects the sample and the compositor falls back to black margins. (raw\.githubusercontent.com)\
\
Pass symbols.DEFAULT_TILE directly. Update TestDungeonMarginMap to leave the tile struct at that address instead of writing a pointer there. In-grid samples can keep using tile_ptrs.\
